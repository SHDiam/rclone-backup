Privacy Policy

rclone-backup is a personal backup tool used only by its owner.

  • It accesses only files that it created itself in the owner's Google Drive (scope: drive.file).
  • It does not collect, sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • Data moves only between the owner's computer and the owner's own Google Drive.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
